# Deck Source Evidence Contract

## Required Source Brief Sections

- `## Evidence list`
- `## Research selection notes`
- `## Image candidates`
- `## Image and asset decisions`
- `## Slide-visible claims`
- `## Speaker-note context`
- `## Unresolved risks`

## Evidence Rules

- Current or source-sensitive topics require at least 8 trusted URLs.
- When official docs exist, include at least 5 official documentation URLs.
- Every slide-visible claim must map to a source.
- Every `slide-spec.json` evidence URL must appear in `source.md`.
- Prefer official, primary, government, institutional, and reputable publication sources.
- Reject SEO summaries, unverifiable screenshots, low-context blog posts, and loosely related stock imagery.

## Output Contract

Researcher reports must separate:

- facts safe for slide-visible text
- context safe only for speaker notes
- image candidates
- rejected weak sources
- unresolved evidence gaps
